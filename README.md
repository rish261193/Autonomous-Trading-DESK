# Autonomous Trading DESK

Research-first AI cryptocurrency trading desk.

## Initial objective
Discover, backtest, and paper-trade BTC/USD and ETH/USD spot strategies, prioritizing net profitability after costs.

## Design principles
- Eleven specialist roles, supported by specialized research and quantitative tools.
- Autonomous Strategy Research Lab for hypothesis generation, screening, and independent validation.
- Independent deterministic risk controls before execution.
- Paper trading only until performance and operational reliability are validated.
- Never commit credentials or API keys.

## Status
Initial repository setup. No live trading enabled.
