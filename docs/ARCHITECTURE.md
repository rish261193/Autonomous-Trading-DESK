# Approved Architecture

## North Star
Sustainable, verifiable **net trading profitability after all costs**, with future subscription commercialization contingent on evidence.

## Eleven specialist roles
1. Market Intelligence — discover market opportunities and hypotheses
2. Technical Analyst — interpret price structure and tool-produced indicators
3. Quant Analyst — design experiments, test hypotheses, measure robustness
4. Fundamental Analyst — evaluate relevant macro, news and crypto fundamentals
5. Bull Agent — argue strongest evidence for long exposure
6. Bear Agent — challenge long exposure and identify adverse conditions
7. Portfolio Manager — rank approved opportunities by expected net return within risk limits
8. Risk Controller — independent, deterministic and non-overridable trade gate
9. Execution Agent — deterministic order placement and reconciliation
10. Performance Auditor — broker-confirmed net P&L and strategy attribution
11. WhatsApp Relationship Manager — reports, explanations, authenticated operational requests; no risk override

## Layers
- **AI Intelligence:** event-driven specialist analysis, debate, bounded and versioned strategy authorization.
- **Tool Capability Layer:** market data, indicators, market regimes, research, vectorized screening, backtests, optimization, and news; use Wendy only after coverage/cost/quality evaluation.
- **Autonomous Strategy Research Lab:** discover -> formalize -> screen -> independently validate -> paper deploy -> audit -> improve.
- **Independent Risk Gateway:** validates every trade; no AI bypass.
- **Fast Python Engine:** monitors signals and executes only authorized strategies without waiting for LLM calls on every tick.
- **Persistent State/Audit:** timestamps, data provenance, strategy versions, decisions, orders, fills, fees, and errors.
- **WhatsApp CRM:** separate from execution authority.

## Safety boundary
Paper trading only for the initial project. No live-money functionality is enabled in the bootstrap. Never store credentials in Git.
